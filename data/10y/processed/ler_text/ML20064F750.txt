Abstract (Limit to 1400 spaces, i.e., approximately 14 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On August 5, 2019, at 09:36, the Control Room was notified that a Secondary Containment Door was found with door hinges broken
and the door was unable to be closed.
Grand Gulf Nuclear Station performed action A 1 for Technical Specification (TS) LCO 3.6.4.1, Secondary Containment, not met
due to the Secondary Containment door being inoperable. The broken hinges were replaced with qualified spares, and TS LCO
3.6.4.1 was met on August 5, 2019 at 13:36.
Based on vendor's engineering analysis, the Direct Cause was Door 1T101A4018 hinge pins failed due to brittle fracture,
resulting in a loss of the Secondary Containment Safety Function.
The Root Cause for the event is a design deficiency related to the mounting of the door hinges. The planned corrective action is a
modification to the door. This will be done when secondary containment is not required to be operable.
This report is made pursuant to 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(C) for an event or condition that at the time of discovery could have
prevented the fulfillment of the safety function of structures or systems that are needed to control the release of radioactive material.
There were no consequences to the general safety of the public, nuclear safety, industrial safety and radiological safety for
this event.

NARRATIVE
Plant Conditions:
Grand Gulf Nuclear Station (GGNS) Unit 1 was operating at approximately 100 percent power in MODE 1. There were no
structures, systems, or components that were inoperable that contributed to the event.
Description:
On August 5, 2019, at 09:36, the Control Room was notified that the Secondary Containment Door (NF) 1T101A401 B (or 1A401 B)
was found with door hinges broken and the door was unable to be closed.
With the Secondary Containment Door unsecured, Secondary Containment was declared INOPERABLE and Secondary
Containment Technical Specification LCO 3.6.4.1 was not met. TS LCO 3.6.4.1 was met on August 5, 2019 at 13:36 and
Secondary Containment was declared operable.
Door 1A 401 B is a security door, and the on-shift Security Force initiated compensatory measures immediately. Therefore, the
Security Plan was not affected.
Door 1A 401 B is a fire barrier. Technical Requirements Manual (TRM) 6.2.8 Condition A was not met. Operations declared the door
NON-FUNCTIONAL and established an hourly fire watch per TRM 6.2.8 Action A.2.2.
TS LCO 3.6.4.1, and TRM 6.2.8 function were met when maintenance technicians replaced the broken hinges for the door and the
door was declared operable on August 5, 2019, at 13:36.
Reportability:
This event was reported as an event or condition that, at the time of discovery could have prevented the fulfillment of the safety
function of structures or systems that are needed to control the release of radioactive material per 1O CFR 50.72(b)(3)(v)(C). The
event notification is EN 54201.
This event is also reportable per 1O CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(C).
Direct Cause:
I
Based on vendor's engineering analysis, the Direct Cause was Door 1T101 A401 B hinge pins failed due to brittle fracture,
resulting in a loss of the Secondary Containment Safety Function.
Root Cause:
The Root Cause for this event is the unique design of the hinges, which introduces a side-loaded stress on the hinge pins while the
door is open. The hinges on the Door 1A 401 B are surface-mounted cam-lift hinges that are designed to be installed in a full
mortised configuration.
Immediate Corrective Actions:
Security Force members initiated compensatory measures immediately while Door 1A 401 B was unable to perform its security
barrier function, satisfying the Security Plan requirements.

Safety Significance:
There were no actual consequences for this event. There was no radiological release from the Secondary Containment as a result

Previous Similar Event:
A three-year review of similar events was conducted.
On July 9, 2017, a pressure boundary door to the Control Room Envelope was left unsecured for approximately one minute. The
Control Room Envelope was declared inoperable, due to the open boundary door. The direct cause of the event was that the door
was degraded, resulting in the door dragging on the floor such that the automatic door closure device would not function properly.
The event was reported as a Loss of Safety Function per 1O CFR 50.73(a)(2){v)(D).
LER 05-416/2017-005-00.
