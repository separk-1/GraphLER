Abstract (Limit to 1400 spaces, 1e , approXJmately 14 single-spaced typewntten lines)
On September 20, 2019, one of four channels for the 3A reactor recirculation pump high differential pressure
function failed the channel functional test. Technical Specification 3.3.5.1, "ECCS Instrumentation," requires
all four channels to be operable. Technical Specification 3.3.5.1, Condition C identifies a Completion Time of
24 hours to restore the failed channel to operable status. The degraded differential pressure switch was
replaced. The channel was restored to operable within the 24 hour time limit.
On June 20, 2019, the Unit 3 East Low Pressure Coolant Injection Room cooler was found leaking water onto
the panel where recirculation pump differential pressure switches are installed. On July 30, 2019, a
surveillance documented that all four channels were functional. The failed channel was determined to be
inoperable between July 30 and September 20, 2019, which is longer than the 24 hours allowed by Technical
Specification. This event is being reported pursuant to 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(i)(B), a condition which was
prohibited by the plant's Technical Specifications. The cause of the degraded differential pressure switch was
due to water dripping from the Low Pressure Coolant Injection room cooler onto the instrumentation rack.
Service Water is isolated to the Unit 3 East Low Pressure Coolant Injection Room cooler until the room cooler
is repaired.

D. Safety Analysis:
Recirculation Pump Differential Pressure signals are used by the LPCI Loop Select Logic to
determine if either recirculat_ion pump is running. Recirculation Pump Differential Pre$si.Jre -
High (i,e., Break Detection) signals are initiated from eight differential pressure switches,
four of which sense the pressure differential between the suction and discharge of each
reactor recirculation pump.
The LPCI System initiation logic contains LPCI Loop Select Logic to identify and direct LPCI
flow to the unbroken recirculation loop if a OBA occurs. The LPCI Loop Select Logic is
initiated upon the receipt of an Emergency Core Cooling System [~E] initiation signal. When
initiated, the LPCI Loop Select Logic first determines recirculation pump operation by ',
sensing the differential pressure between the suction and discharge of each pump.
