Abstract (Limit to 1400 spaces, i.e., approximately 14 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On July 2, 2018, at 1122 Central Daylight Time, it was discovered that the last performed surveillance on the C
Emergency Diesel Generator (DG) Battery had recorded the specific gravity of Battery Cell 20 at 1.204. This was
0.025 less than the average battery cell specific gravity of 1.229. The difference in specific gravity between the
individual cell and the average cell was more than the BFN Unit 1 and Unit 2 Technical Specifications (TS) Limiting
Conditions for Operation (LCO) 3.8.6, Table 3.8.6-1, Category C, limit of 0.020.
On August 3, 2018, a past operability evaluation determined that, because of this condition, the C DG had been
inoperable for longer than the LCO 3.8.1 Condition B, 14 day allowed outage time. Also, during this time, there were
two periods where a second Unit 1 and 2 DG was inoperable for longer than the two hours allowed by TS LCO 3.8.1
Condition H for two or more concurrently inoperable Unit 1 and 2 DGs. This condition resulted in a violation of both
Unit 1 and 2 TS.
The cause of this event is that the TS 3.8.6 Surveillance Requirement (SR) is non-conservative in regard to checking
Category C limits. No SR is specified in TS 3.8.6 to check Category C limits. Corrective actions are to replace the
battery cell, to issue an Operations Standing Order ensuring TS 3.8.6 Category C limits are met when quarterly SRs
are performed on DG batteries, to revise battery SR procedures, and to revise Unit 1, 2, and 3 TS 3.8 to address the
discrepancy between the SR and the TS 3.8.6 LCO Condition B required action.

I. Plant Operating Conditions Before the Event
At the time of discovery, Browns Ferry Nuclear Plant (BFN), Units 1 and 2, were in Mode 1 at
approximately 100 percent rated thermal power.
II. Description of Event
A. Event Summary
On July 2, 2018, at 1122 Central Daylight Time (CDT), it was discovered that the last
performed surveillance on the C Emergency Diesel Generator (DG)[EK][DG]
Battery[BTRY] had recorded the specific gravity of Battery Cell 20 at 1.204. This was
0.025 less than the average battery cell specific gravity of 1.229. The difference in
specific gravity between the individual cell and the average cell was more than the BFN
Unit 1 and Unit 2 Technical Specifications (TS) Limiting Conditions for Operation (LCO)
3.8.6, Table 3.8.6-1, Category C, limit of 0.020.
On August 3, 2018, a Past Operability Evaluation (POE) determined that, because of this
condition, the C DG had been inoperable for longer than the LCO 3.8.1 Condition B, 14
day allowed outage time. Also, during this time, there were two periods where a second
Unit 1 and 2 DG was inoperable for longer than the two hours allowed by TS LCO 3.8.1
Condition H for two or more concurrently inoperable Unit 1/2 DGs. This condition
resulted in a violation of both Unit 1 and 2 TS.
The Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) is submitting this report in accordance with
10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(i)(B), as any event or condition which was prohibited by the plant TS.
B. Status of structures, components, or systems that were inoperable at the start of
the event and that contributed to the event

time of discovery, which contributed to this condition.

G. Failure mode, mechanism, and effect of each failed component
The battery cell is considered failed because of a trend of lowering specific gravity
compared to the average for that battery. This resulted in the past TS inoperability of the
battery when the difference in specific gravity between the cell and the battery average
was found to have been greater than 0.020.
H. Operator actions
There were no operator actions associated with this condition
I. Automatically and manually initiated safety system responses
There were no safety system responses initiated as a result of this condition.
III. Cause of the Event
A. Cause of each component or system failure or personnel error
The direct cause of this condition is a downtrend in C DG Battery Cell 20 specific gravity.
Additionally, two contributing causes were identified:
1. The common interpretation of TS 3.8.6 is that Category A and Category B limits
must be exceeded prior to checking Category C limits.
2. Procedure 0 SR 3.8.6.2(DG C), "Quarterly check of Diesel Generator C Battery,"
does not perform a check of TS 3.8.6 Category C limits.
‐ ‐ ‐
B. Cause(s) and circumstances for each human performance related root cause
The apparent cause of this event was that the TS 3.8.6 SR is non-conservative in regard
to checking Category C limits. No Surveillance Requirements are specified in TS 3.8.6 to
check Category C limits.
IV. Analysis of the Event
The safety objective of the Standby AC Power System is to provide a self-contained, highly
reliable source of power, as required for the Engineered Safeguards System, so that no single
credible event can disable the core standby cooling functions or their supporting auxiliaries.
Control circuit voltage for the DGs is 125-V DC. Each DG has its own battery. The diesel
batteries are located in the DG Building. Each battery consists of 60 single cell containers. TS
3.8 (Electrical Power Systems) Table 3.8.6-1, Category C, gives the requirement that the specific
gravity for each connected battery cell be not more than 0.020 below the average specific gravity
of all connected cells.

Quarterly Surveillance Procedure 0-SR-3.8.6.2(DG-A/B/C/D) is the procedure for performing
Surveillance Requirements 3.8.6.1 (Verify battery cell parameters meet Table 3.8.6-1 Category A
limits), 3.8.6.2 (Verify battery cell parameters meet Table 3.8.6-1 Category B limits), and 3.8.6.3
(Verify average electrolyte temperature of representative cells is greater than or equal to 60
degrees F for each Unit and Shutdown Board [ECBD] battery [except Shutdown Board battery
3EB], and greater than or equal to 40 degrees F for Shutdown Board battery 3EB and each DG
battery). Neither this procedure nor its parent SRs require plant personnel to verify that battery
cell parameters meet Table 3.8.6-1 Category C limits, and it was found to be commonly assumed
by plant personnel that Category A and Category B limits must be exceeded prior to checking
Category C limits.
TS LCO 3.8.6 requires that battery cell parameters for the Unit, Shutdown, and DG batteries
remain within the limits of Table 3.8.6-1 when the associated DC electrical power subsystems
are required to be operable. TS LCO 3.8.6 Condition B states that if one or more batteries
contain battery cells with parameters not within Category C values, the associated battery must
immediately be declared inoperable.
TS LCO 3.8.4 requires that Unit 1 and 2 DG DC subsystems be operable in Modes 1, 2, and 3.
TS LCO 3.8.4 Condition C states that, with one or more DG DC electrical power subsystems
inoperable (including the DG battery), the associated DG must immediately be declared
inoperable.
TS LCO 3.8.1 requires that Unit 1 and 2 DGs (DGs A, B, C, and D) be operable in Modes 1, 2,
and 3. TS LCO 3.8.1 Condition B states that, with one required Unit 1 and 2 DG inoperable, the
following actions must be taken:
• Verify power availability from the offsite transmission network within one hour and once
per eight hours thereafter; AND
• Evaluate the availability of both temporary diesel generators (TDGs) within one hour and
once per twelve hours thereafter; AND
• Declare required feature(s), supported by the inoperable Unit 1 and 2 DG, inoperable
when the redundant required feature(s) are inoperable within four hours of the discovery
of Condition B concurrent with inoperability of redundant required feature(s) ; AND
• Determine operable Unit 1 and 2 DG(s) are not inoperable due to common cause failure
or perform SR 3.8.1.1 for operable Unit 1 and 2 DG(s) within twenty-four hours; AND
• Restore inoperable Unit 1 and 2 DG to operable status within fourteen days, and within
seven days from the discovery of unavailable TDG(s), and within twenty-four hours from
the discovery of Condition B entry greater than six days concurrent with unavailability of
TDG(s), and within twenty-one days of discovery of the failure to meet the LCO.

TS LCO 3.8.1 Condition H states that, with two required Unit 1 and 2 DGs inoperable, action
must be taken to restore all but one Unit 1 and 2 DG to operable status. During the time when C
DG was inoperable, there were two periods where B DG was inoperable for longer than two
hours.
TS LCO 3.8.1 Condition I states that if required actions and completion times are not met for
Condition A, B, C, D, E, F, or H, action must be taken to be in Mode 3 within twelve hours, and to
be in Mode 4 within thirty-six hours.
A review of surveillances performed on June 6, 2018, determined that the specific gravity of C
DG Battery Cell 20 was 1.204. This was 0.025 below the average cell specific gravity of 1.229,
and did not meet the requirements of Table 3.8.6-1. Therefore, the C DG Battery was inoperable
by LCO 3.8.6, and the C DG was inoperable by LCO 3.8.4. A POE determined that, because of
a declining trend in Cell 20 specific gravity, the battery should be considered inoperable from the
date of its last satisfactory surveillance on April 10, 2018, to June 4, 2018, when the DG was
declared inoperable for its planned maintenance window. During this time, the Required Actions
of TS LCO 3.8.6 Condition B; TS LCO 3.8.4 Condition C; and TS LCO 3.8.1 Conditions B, H, and
I were not completed. Therefore, BFN was in violation of its TS during this time.
V. Assessment of Safety Consequences
A POE was performed for this condition which determined that, even with Cell 20 not meeting the
TS LCO 3.8.6 Category C value for individual cell specific gravity, the entire battery bank was
sufficient to supply the power necessary for DG C to perform its safety function for its seven-day
mission time.
A probabilistic risk assessment was performed for this condition which determined that this
condition was of low safety significance.
A. Availability of systems or components that could have performed the same
function as the components and systems that failed during the event
During the period of time when this condition existed, offsite power was continually
available to the Unit 1 and Unit 2 4kV Shutdown Boards.
Additionally, three Unit 1 and 2 DGs were available with the following exceptions:
• B DG inoperable from 1044 CDT on April 23, 2018, to 1113 CDT on May 03,
2018.
• B DG inoperable from 0926 CDT on May 22, 2018, to 1330 CDT on May 23,
2018.

VI. Corrective Actions
This condition was entered into the TVA Corrective Action Program (CAP) and is being tracked
under Condition Report 1427962.
A. Immediate Corrective Actions
1. Replace battery cell 20.
2. Issue Operations Standing Order, OS-201, to verify TS 3.8.6 Category C limits are
met when reviewing quarterly battery surveillances for Main Bank Batteries 1, 2,
and 3; Shutdown Board Batteries A, B, C, D, 3EB; Unit 1 and 2 Diesel Generator
Batteries; and Unit 3 Diesel Generator Batteries.
B. Corrective Actions to Prevent Recurrence or to reduce probability of similar events
occurring in the future
1. Revise battery SR procedures to add a note that Category C specific gravity limits

VII. Previous Similar Events at the Same Site
A POE performed for extent-of-condition determined that during the past three years, there have
been thirty-three instances where required 125-V or 250-V battery cells have failed to meet TS
Table 3.8.6-1 Category C requirements. No adverse trend was observed because, for each
incident identified, the previous and subsequent recorded values were within TS requirements
and there were no indications of lowering specific gravity. Since there is no adverse trend, time
of discovery is used for the purpose of determining past operability. Therefore, these instances
do not constitute violations of BFN TS.
