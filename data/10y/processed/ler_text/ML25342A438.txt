16. Abstract (Limit to 1326 spaces, i.e., approximately 13 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On October 9, 2025, at 1207 CDT, River Bend Station (RBS) was operating at 100% reactor power when a manual
reactor trip was inserted as a result of a Division I Balance of Plant (BOP) isolation. The BOP isolation resulted in the loss
of the Instrument Air System (IAS) and the Component Cooling Primary System (CCP) to containment. All plant systems
responded as designed.
Immediately following the manual trip, a reactor water low level 3 isolation signal was received as expected. Due to the
swell from the main turbine trip, a reactor water high level 8 actuation signal was received at 1208 CDT. The level 8
actuation signal was reset at 1211 CDT.
At 1237 CDT, Control Room Operators entered the Emergency Operating Procedure (EOP) for high containment
pressure caused by the isolation of the containment vent flow path. The isolation signal for IAS and CCP was reset at
1255 CDT. Following the restoration of the isolation signal, Control Room Operators lowered containment pressure and
exited the EOP at 1305 CDT.
The direct cause of this event was a failed fuse caused by a human performance error.
This report is made pursuant to 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(iv)(A), any event or condition that resulted in manual or automatic
actuation of the Reactor Protection System.

NARRATIVE
Event Description
On October 9, 2025, at 1207 CDT, with River Bend Station (RBS), Unit 1 operating at 100% reactor power, a manual
reactor trip was inserted as a result of a Division I Balance of Plant (BOP) isolation. The Division 1 BOP isolation resulted
in the loss of the Instrument Air System (IAS) [LO] and the Component Cooling Primary System (CCP) [CC] to
containment.
On October 9, 2025, at 1203 CDT, a Division 1 BOP isolation signal was received. Control Room operators recognized
the loss of IAS and CCP to containment and entered the appropriate procedures. The Control Room operators were
unable to reset the Division 1 BOP isolation signal. At 1207 CDT, the Control Room Operators inserted a manual reactor
trip by placing the Mode Switch to Shutdown. All plant systems responded as designed.
Immediately following the manual trip, a low reactor water level isolation signal was received as expected. Due to the
swell from the Main Turbine [TA] trip, a high reactor water level actuation signal was received at 1208 CDT. The high
reactor water level actuation caused the Reactor Feedwater System (FWS) [SJ] to trip as designed. The high reactor
water level actuation signal was reset at 1211 CDT and Reactor Feedwater Pump C (FWS-P1 C) was restarted to
maintain reactor water level. Reactor pressure was maintained by the Turbine Bypass Valves [JI].
At 1237 CDT, Control Room operators entered the Emergency Operating Procedure (EOP) for high containment
pressure. The increase in containment pressure was due to the isolation of the containment vent flow path. At 1255
CDT, the isolation signal for IAS and CCP was reset. Following the restoration of the isolation signal, Control Room
operators lowered containment pressure and exited the EOP at 1305 CDT.
This event was reported on October 9, 2025, at 1539 CDT in accordance with 10 CFR 50.72(b)(2)(iv)(B) and 10 CFR
50.72(b)(3)(iv)(A) as an event or condition that results in actuation of the RPS when the reactor is critical and as a
specified system actuation due to the expected low reactor water level isolation signal immediately following the
reactor scram and a subsequent high reactor water level signal (EN 57975).
Event Cause
Prior to the Division 1 BOP isolation, plant personnel were performing surveillance STP-058-4201, Containment and
Drywell Manual Isolation Actuation Logic System Functional Test. During the surveillance, a technician lifted a lead and
did not appropriately account for the length of wire allowing the wire to retract and contact the metal enclosure. This
caused the Division 1 Isolation Logic Supply Fuse (B21 H-F28A) [JM] to fail. Due to the failure of B21 H-F28A, a Division 1
BOP isolation actuation occurred. The Division 1 BOP isolation resulted in the loss of the IAS and CCP to containment.
Safety Assessment
The actual consequence of this event was a Division 1 BOP containment isolation that was unable to be reset and a
subsequent reactor scram. There were no other actual consequences to general safety of the public, nuclear safety,
industrial safety and radiological safety for this event.
Corrective Actions
Planned:
• Complete performance management with personnel involved to address personal accountability.
• Develop and implement a non-training intervention plan to reinforce standards and expectations.

Previous Occurrences
LER-2025-001-00, Human Performance Error Results in Manual Reactor Trip
Plant personnel failed to close SWP-V3302, SWP-MOV55A block valve, prior to post maintenance testing. During
post maintenance testing, maintenance personnel requested Operations to open SWP-MOV55A, Standby Cooling
Tower Division 1 Return Isolation Valve, as the valve opened all normal service water pumps received a low-pressure
lockout condition due to an open flow path to the Standby Cooling Tower. This resulted in a loss of Normal Service
Water and a subsequent manual reactor trip.
LER-2023-006-00, Automatic Reactor Scram Due to a Turbine Trip Following a Disconnect Failure at Switchyard
The cause of the turbine trip signal was due to a Generator Side Disconnect switch failing as a result of not being fully
seated in the saddle of the disconnect fingers. After further investigation, it was determined that the disconnects were
not properly seated in the saddle of the disconnect fingers due to personnel error.
