16. Abstract (Limit to 1326 spaces, i.e., approximately 13 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On July 14, 2025, at 12:54 CT, it was identified that the flow recorder [FR] for the Standby Gas Treatment System
(SGTS) B train was providing inaccurate information while SGTS B was in standby. SGTS B was determined to be
inoperable. A failed flow switch was replaced and SGTS B operability was restored on July 14, 2025.
On July 24, 2025, analysis determined that the flow recorder [FR] began receiving inaccurate information on June 21,
2025. This resulted in a condition that was in violation of Technical Specification (TS) 3.6.4.3, Condition A which states
that when one SGTS train is inoperable, it must be restored to operable status within seven days.
The failure to identify the prohibited condition was caused by operator rounds procedures not having a task to review the
SGTS flow. An action has been created to update the relevant procedures.
This report is being made in accordance with 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(i)(B) for any condition which was prohibited by the
plants Technical Specifications.

NARRATIVE
Plant Conditions:
At the time of the event, Grand Gulf Nuclear Station (GGNS) Unit 1 was in MODE 1 at 100 percent power and all safety
systems were operable.
Description of Event:
On July 14, 2025, at 12:54 CT, it was identified that the flow recorder [FR] for the Standby Gas Treatment System
(SGTS) B train was providing inaccurate information while SGTS B was in standby. SGTS B was determined to be
inoperable. A failed flow switch was replaced and SGTS B operability was restored on July 14, 2025.
On July 24, 2025, analysis determined that the flow recorder [FR] began receiving inaccurate information on June 21,
2025. This resulted in a condition that was in violation of Technical Specification (TS) 3.6.4.3, Condition A which states
that when one SGTS train is inoperable, it must be restored to operable status within seven days.
During this time, SGTS A was operable, and capable of fulfilling the SGTS safety function.
Reportability:
This report is being made in accordance with 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(i)(B) for any condition which was prohibited by the
plants Technical Specifications.
Cause(s):
The failure to identify the prohibited condition was caused by operator rounds procedures not having a task to review
the SGTS flow. The cause of the failure to properly detect the inaccurate data and failed flow switch was due to the lack of a
task to review the SGTS flow in operator rounds.
Corrective Actions:
- Replace the faulty filter train B flow low switch. Action completed.
- Update 02-S-01-31 Control Room Rounds procedure to include recording values from low flow switches. Action in
progress.
Safety Significance:
There were no actual safety consequences associated with this condition. Plant systems responded as designed.
