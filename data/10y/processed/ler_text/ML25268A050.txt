16. Abstract (Limit to 1326 spaces, i.e., approximately 13 single-spaced typewritten lines)
At 22:00 on July 28, 2025, with Monticello Nuclear Generating Plant (MNGP) operating at 100 percent power,
secondary containment was declared inoperable due to a Reactor Building Ventilation System failure which
caused a degradation of Reactor Building differential pressure that exceeded the Technical Specification limits.
This Ventilation System failure led to elevated temperatures in the Steam Chase. Operators began to lower
Reactor power to attempt to slow the rising Steam Chase temperature level. At 23:24 on July 28, 2025, with
MNGP now operating at 41 percent power, the Reactor was manually scrammed due to Steam Chase
temperatures reaching the procedural limit. Operators responded to the trip and stabilized the plant in
accordance with approved procedures and safely placed the plant in Mode 3. All systems responded normally
post trip. The Ventilation System failure was caused by rainwater intrusion through a leak in the plenum room
roof that made contact with and then tripped energized Ventilation System components. The entire plenum
room roof is in the process of being replaced. Immediate actions were taken to prevent rainwater intrusion
from impacting Ventilation System components while the roof is being replaced.

NARRATIVE
EVENT DESCRIPTION AND ANALYSIS
On the evening of July 28, 2025, Monticello Nuclear Generating Plant (MNGP) was operating at 100 percent power when
a thunderstorm passed over the area. As the storm passed, rainwater began dripping through the roofing material into
the plenum room which is located between the Reactor Building and Turbine Building. The water ran down the wall above
cabinet N1131, V-EF-24 LOCAL CONTACTOR. At about 20:58 on July 28, 2025, this leak in the plenum roof led to an
electrical short of a contactor for motor V-EF-24, REACTOR BUILDING EXHAUST FAN (Reliance Electric, model BFC
SO7827-1) [VL]. This short caused a fuse in a General Electric 7700 line breaker B1131, V-EF-24A,B, REACTOR
BUILDING MAIN EXHAUST FANS 480 VAC, to open resulting in a loss of power to both Reactor Building main exhaust
fans. Then, V-AC-10A and V-AC-10B, REACTOR BUILDING MAIN SUPPLY HVAC UNITS, [VA] tripped as designed with
the V-EF-24 flow interlock.
With the failure of Reactor Building Exhaust Ventilation and then the subsequent trip of the Reactor Building Supply
Ventilation, this resulted in a degradation of the Reactor Building differential pressure. At 22:00, on July 28, 2025, with
MNGP still operating at 100 percent power, the differential pressure exceeded the Technical Specifications limit resulting
in Secondary Containment being declared as inoperable.
With the failure of the Ventilation System, Steam Chase temperatures started to rise. Operators began to lower Reactor
power to attempt to slow the rising Steam Chase temperature level.
At 23:24 on July 28, 2025, with MNGP now operating at a reduced power of 41 percent, the Operators inserted a manual
scram of the Reactor due to the Steam Chase temperatures reaching the procedural limit of 180 degrees Fahrenheit per
the criteria in the Ventilation System procedure.
This event is reportable per 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(C) and 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(D) for an event or condition that could
have prevented the fulfillment of the safety function of structures or systems that are needed to control the release of
radioactive material; and mitigate the consequences of an accident, and per 10 CFR 50.73(a)(2)(iv)(A) due to actuation of
the Reactor Protection System.
ASSESSMENT OF SAFETY CONSEQUENCES
The scram was not complex, with all systems responding normally post scram. Reactor water level was maintained via
the Feedwater System. Decay heat was removed by discharging steam to the main condenser using the turbine bypass
valves.
The health and safety of the public and site personnel were not impacted during this event.
CAUSE OF THE EVENT
The Ventilation System failure was caused by rainwater intrusion through a leak in the plenum room roof that then
made contact with and tripped energized Ventilation System components

NARRATIVE
CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
Immediately after the event, sealant was applied to electrical cabinets in the plenum room to prevent rainwater intrusion
from impacting the Ventilation System components.
To reduce the likelihood of this event happening again, replacement of the entire plenum roof is currently in progress.
MNGP was placed back online at 08:30 on August 2, 2025.
PREVIOUS SIMILAR EVENTS
There were no previous similar events in the past three years.
