16. Abstract (Limit to 1326 spaces, i.e., approximately 13 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On September 19th, 2025, the 13 kV non-safety related AS4 feeder breaker, which powers the B Cooling Tower Lift Pump
(EIIS:NN) tripped as a result of an electrical fault downstream of the breaker. The electrical fault resulted in a momentary
voltage dip on the offsite power source (3SU). This condition led to a partial invalid Primary Containment Isolation System
(PCIS) (EIIS: JM) isolation including isolation of the Reactor Water Cleanup System (RWCU) (EIIS: CE) on both units. The
cause of the breaker trip was a faulted disconnect switch, likely due to component aging.
This condition is reportable under 50.73(a)(2)(iv)(A) as an invalid actuation of general containment isolation signals affecting
containment isolation valves in more than one system. The isolation was caused by degraded voltage. The actuation was
invalid because the conditions for which isolation is required were not present.

NARRATIVE
Plant Operating Conditions Before the Event
Prior to the event, Peach Bottom Atomic Power Station, Unit 2 was operating at approximately 100% power in
MODE 1. Unit 3 was operating at approximately 91% power in MODE 1 in end-of-cycle coast down. There
were no structures, systems or components (SSC) that were inoperable at the start of the event that
contributed to the event.
Event Description
On September 19th, 2025, at approximately 9:34am, operators received alarms indicating that the 480V motor
control center that supplies support power to the B Cooling Tower (EIIS: NN) lift pump tripped and caused
various isolations on both units. On Unit 2, the voltage reduction caused a partial invalid Primary Containment
Isolation System (PCIS) (EIIS: JM) isolation of Group II and Group III inboard valves including isolation of the
Reactor Water Cleanup System (RWCU) (EIIS: CE), isolation of nitrogen compressors (EIIS: LK), failure of the
B vent stack radiation monitor (EIIS: IL), and loss of flow to containment hydrogen and oxygen analyzers (EIIS:
IK). On Unit 3, this condition resulted in actuations of PCIS Group II and III outboard valves, isolation of
RWCU, isolation of nitrogen compressors and a trip of a service water booster pump (EIIS: DA). The A train of
flow indication to the Main Stack sampler (EIIS: IL) was also lost.
The cause of the breaker trip was a faulted disconnect switch, likely due to component aging and general
corrosion. The failed switch is located outdoors and was replaced in 2007 after a similar failure. Preventive
maintenance had not been performed since the 2007 failure.
This condition is reportable under 50.73(a)(2)(iv)(A) as an invalid actuation of general containment isolation
signals affecting containment isolation valves in more than one system. The isolation was caused by degraded
voltage. The actuation was invalid because the conditions for which isolation is required were not present.
Safety Consequences
There were no challenges to plant safety as a result of this event. Both units responded as designed to the
electrical transient, and PCIS isolations were reset without error within approximately one hour of the trip.
Corrective Actions
Corrective actions for this condition include replacement of the components that were damaged by the fault,
including radiation monitors, transformer disconnect switch and cabling. The maintenance strategy for this
equipment will be reviewed.
Previous Similar Events
Peach Bottom experienced the following similar events:
- Invalid actuations due to a trip of the AS4 feeder breaker in 2007, ref. EN# 43910
- Invalid actuations due to an emergency bus breaker relay failure in 2019, ref. LER 2019-001-00.
- Invalid actuations due to a bus failure on August 20, 2025, rev. LER 2025-001-00.
