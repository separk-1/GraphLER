16. Abstract (Limit to 1560 spaces, i.e., approximately 15 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On January 19, 2022 during the performance of the B Emergency Diesel Generator (EDG) 24-hour surveillance test, the B EDG 
did not achieve the required load of 4652 kW-4873 kW (105 – 110 percent of rated load).  The maximum load achieved was 
4650 kW (104.9 percent).  The B EDG was determined to be inoperable due to failing to meet the acceptance criteria.  
Investigation identified that the B EDG fuel supply linkage mechanism (fuel rack) required adjustment.  Following adjustment, 
the B EDG was re-tested satisfactorily.  The B EDG was returned to operable status on January 23, 2022 at 0700.   
Technical Specification (TS) 3.8.1.1 allows the B EDG to remain inoperable for up to 14 days.  Based on a review of the B EDG 
maintenance history, it was determined that the condition existed prior to discovery and for a period greater than 14 days.  
Therefore, an Operation or Condition Prohibited by Technical Specifications occurred, and this report is being made per the 
requirements of 10CFR50.73(a)(2)(i)(B).

IDENTIFICATION OF OCCURRENCE 
Event Date: May 3, 2021 
Discovery Date: January 19, 2022 
CONDITIONS PRIOR TO OCCURRENCE 
Hope Creek was in Operational Condition (OPCON) 1, at 100 percent rated thermal power.  No other structures, systems 
or components that could have contributed to the event were inoperable at the time of the event. 
DESCRIPTION OF OCCURRENCE 
On January 19, 2022 Hope Creek was performing TS surveillance requirement 4.8.1.1.2.k, which requires that the B 
Emergency Diesel Generator (EDG) be operated for 22 hours at rated load, followed by 2 hours at a load of 4652 kW - 
4873 kW  (105 to 110 percent of rated load ).   This test is performed once per 20.5 months .  Following successful 
operation at rated load for 22 hours, the maximum load that could be achieved was 4650 kW for the final portion of the 
surveillance test.  Based on this condition, the B EDG was determined to be inoperable on January 19, 2022 at 12:59 . 
An investigation determined that the B EDG cylinder injection pumps needed adjustment to increase fueling to the 
engine. This is performed by adjusting a mechanical linkage (fuel rack).   Following adjustment, the EDG was re-tested 
successfully and declared operable.   
CAUSE 
An investigation of the maintenance history determined that the inability of the B EDG to reach max load condition was 
introduced when fuel rack adjustments were made in April and May, 2021 during planned maintenance conducted during 
the Hope Creek refuel outage 23.   Following the adjustments, the EDG was tested to 4000-4400 kW per TS surveillance 
requirement 4.8.1.1.2.a, on May 3, 2021 . It was not tested at the higher load of 105-110 percent. The condition on the B 
EDG existed from May 3, 2021 until resolved on January 23, 2022.  This exceeds the TS allowed outage time of 14 days. 
SAFETY CONSEQUENCES 
Per the Hope Creek Updated Final Safety Analysis Report (UFSAR) and supporting calculations, the maximum EDG 
loading for any of the EDGs under accident conditions is 4628.44 kW.   Under the condition described above, the B EDG 
was able to be loaded to 4650 kW, therefore the B EDG was able to satisfy the design basis loading requirements.    
PREVIOUS EVENTS 
A review of station Licensee Event Reports and the corrective action program for the past 3 years was conducted. No 
LERs were identified . One corrective action occurrence was identified.  In October, 2019 the C EDG would not achieve 
more than 4700 kW following a planned governor replacement. The condition was identified during post maintenance 
testing and was corrected prior to returning the EDG to operable status.

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS 
On January 22, 2022, the B EDG fuel rack mechanical linkage was adjusted to allow the required loading of 4652 kW - 
4873 kW to be achieved.   
The remaining three EDGs (A, C and D) were verified to have successfully completed TS surveillance requirement 
4.8.1.1.2.k (105 - 110 percent loaded run) subsequent to refueling outage 23. A review of maintenance activities found 
no history of fuel rack adjustments since the last successful completion of surveillance requirement 4.8.1.1.2.k. 
A cause evaluation is in progress to identify additional actions.
