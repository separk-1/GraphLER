Abstract (Limit to 1400 spaces, i.e., approximately 14 single-spaced typewritten lines)
On January 30, 2019, the operating heating boiler tripped during a period of extremely cold external air
temperatures. To address the potential of freezing the heating steam coils and filters, procedures provided
direction to open the ventilation inlet plenum doors. At 0910, the Reactor Building differential pressure
degraded and was determined to be below 0.25 inches water column vacuum. Technical Specification (TS)
3.6.4.1, "Secondary Containment," was not met and Condition A, Secondary Containment inoperable, was
required since differential pressure degraded below the 0.25 inches water column vacuum by local average
gauge readings. Standby Gas Treatment was started to assist in differential pressure control. At 0928,
differential pressure improved and was maintaining above 0.25 inches water column vacuum. TS 3.6.4.1
Condition A was exited. This event is reportable under 1O CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(C), "Any event or condition that
could have prevented the fulfillment of the safety function of ... systems that are needed control the release of
radioactive material." The cause of the event was determined to be inadequate procedural guidance for
responding to loss of heating boilers during extreme cold external air conditions. Corrective actions include
revising station procedure to prioritize actions to mitigate ventilation transients during a loss of heater boilers.

PLANT AND SYSTEM IDENTIFICATION
Dresden Nuclear Power Station (DNPS), Unit 2, is a General Electric Company Boiling Water Reactor with a
licensed maximum power level of -2957 megawatts thermal. The Energy Industry Identification System
codes used in the text are identified as [XX].
A. Plant Conditions Prior to Event:
Unit: 02 Event Date: 01/30/2019 Event Time: 091 O CST
Reactor Mode: 1 Mode Name: Power Operation Power Level: 100 percent
B. Description of Event:
On January 30, 2019, the operating heating boiler [BLR] tripped during a period of extremely cold external
air temperatures of approximately -25 degrees Fahrenheit (F). The heating boiler tripped with subsequent
supply low air temperature alarm [TA] at 0850. The heating boiler tripped due to sluggish operation of its
pressure regulator [RG]. Due to the loss of heating steam to the ventilation supply steam coils, the
temperature of the supply air to the Reactor Building (RB) [NG] ventilation [VA] system rapidly decreased,
which began to degrade the ability of RB ventilation to maintain Secondary Containment [NG] differential
pressure (DP). To address the potential of freezing the heating steam coils [HCL] and filters [FLT], station
procedures for loss of heating boilers provided direction to open the RB ventilation inlet plenum doors [DR].
Opening the plenum doors further degraded the ability to maintain RB DP due to additional air supply path
to the plenum vent fan suctions.
At 0900, there was an unexpected low RB differential pressure alarm [PDA]. At 0910, DP readings from the
local gauges located on each wall of the refuel floor were obtained. The average of these RB DP readings
was determined to be below 0.25 inches water column (we) vacuum showing Secondary Containment DP
had degraded. This condition led to not meeting Technical Specification {TS) 3.6.4.1, "Secondary
Containment," and required entering Condition A, "Secondary Containment inoperable in Mode 1, 2, or 3,"
Required Action A.1, "Restore Secondary Containment to OPERABLE status," with a Completion Time of
four hours.
At 0922, the Standby Gas Treatment (SBGT) [BH] system was started. At 0928, Unit 3 RB ventilation
system was secured. RB DP improved and was maintaining above 0.25 inches we vacuum. TS 3.6.4.1
Condition A was exited.
An 8-hour non-emergency notification was made via Emergency Notification System (ENS #53851) in
accordance with 1O CFR 50.72(b){3){v)(C) for the loss of Secondary Containment.

C. Cause of Event:
The cause of the event was determined to be inadequate procedural guidance for responding to loss of
heating boilers during extreme cold external air temperatures.
D. Safety Analysis:
Except for the heating boilers pressure regulator prior to the event, all equipment responded as designed.
Operator action to start SBGT and to secure Unit 3 RB ventilation in accordance with procedure guidance
prevented further degradation of the RB DP. Based on the Secondary Containment DP remaining negative
during this event, the safety function of Secondary Containment was maintained, and this event is of low
safety significance.
This event is being reported under 1O CFR 50.73(a)(2)(v)(C), "Any event or condition that could have
prevented the fulfillment of the safety function of ... systems that are needed control the release of
radioactive material."
E. Corrective Actions:
The operational response procedure for the loss of plant heating steam during extreme external air
temperature conditions will be revised to prioritize Main Control Room operations to mitigate ventilation
transients during a loss of heating boilers.
F. Previous Occurrences:
A previous event, Dresden LER 237/2016-001-00 on docket 50-237, was found during the investigation
where a Secondary Containment DP transient followed a heating boiler shutdown. The Secondary
Containment DP transient reported under this LER 2019-001-00 did not have the same causal factors.
